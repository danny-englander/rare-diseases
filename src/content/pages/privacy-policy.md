This Privacy Policy describes how the Rare Disease Index ("we", "us", or
"our") handles information when you visit this site.

## Information we collect

This site is a static prototype — there are no user accounts, and disease
search and filtering happen entirely in your browser. The only thing this
site stores about you is your light/dark theme preference, saved in your
browser's `localStorage` so it doesn't need to ask again on your next
visit. That preference never leaves your device.

If you use the [contact form](/contact), we receive whatever you enter:
your name, email address, and message content, so we can respond to you.

## How we use information

Contact-form submissions are used only to reply to your message. We don't
sell or share your personal information, and we don't use it for anything
beyond responding to you.

## Third-party services

- **Netlify** hosts this site and processes contact-form submissions.
- **Orphadata** (Orphanet's free, open-access data service) is the source
  of the disease data shown on this site, licensed under
  [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). This site is
  an independently built interface over that data and is not an Orphanet
  product.

We don't run any third-party analytics or advertising trackers on this
site.

## Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please use the
[contact form](/contact).
