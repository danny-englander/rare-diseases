The Rare Disease Index is a prototype search interface for looking up rare
diseases — built with [Astro](https://astro.build/), Tailwind CSS, daisyUI,
and [Pagefind](https://pagefind.app/) for instant client-side search.

It currently covers a sample of the diseases catalogued by
[Orphadata](https://www.orphadata.com/), Orphanet's free, open-access data
service — not the full catalogue. Not finding a disease here doesn't mean
it isn't real or rare; it means the sample hasn't reached it yet.

## What this is for

This project exists to demonstrate a fast, faceted way to search structured
rare-disease data: by name, synonym, or symptom, and filtered by body
system, inheritance pattern, age of onset, or rarity. It's a working
interface built on real data, not a finished product.

## What this isn't

This site is not a substitute for medical advice, and it isn't affiliated
with or endorsed by Orphanet. It's an independently built interface over
data Orphanet makes freely available under a
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) license. If you or
someone you know is dealing with a rare disease, please talk to a
qualified healthcare provider or a patient organization for that condition.
